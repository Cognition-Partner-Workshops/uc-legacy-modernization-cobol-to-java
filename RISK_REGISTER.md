## 4. Top 5 risks — response plans

1. **R-01 Hidden business rules (20)** — Build a *rule catalogue* per hotspot (`COACTUPC`, `CBTRN02C`, `COPAUA0C`, `CBACT04C`, `COTRN02C`) with rule ID, COBOL paragraph, condition, outcome; every rule gets an executable test. Parallel runs catch the remainder.
2. **R-11 SME scarcity (20)** — Treat knowledge capture as a P0 deliverable; schedule SMEs by phase; use the analysis documents and the repo DeepWiki as the shared baseline.
3. **R-03 Data conversion (16)** — Converters are generated from copybooks, not hand-written; conversion is certified with round-trip and checksum tests before any service consumes data.
4. **R-05 Synchronization (16)** — Enforce single-writer per store/field, measure CDC lag, reconcile nightly, and make every cut-over reversible.
5. **R-08 Reference-data divergence (16)** — Retired early by P2, which also proves the "new master publishes legacy extracts" pattern used in later phases.

## 5. Review cadence

- Register reviewed at every phase go/no-go and monthly steering.
- Exposure re-scored with evidence (reconciliation results, parallel-run diff rates, incident data).
- Closed risks retained with closure evidence for audit.
